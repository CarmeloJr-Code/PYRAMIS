### Unified Business Web Portal Design for Purple Yam Malaybalay

Purple Yam Malaybalay's website is a **Unified Business Web Portal** is an all-in-one website that combines a customer-facing **e-commerce storefront** with a secure, role-based **employee workspace** within the same web application.

The public-facing landing page primarily functions as an online shop, allowing customers to browse products and place pre-orders without accounts needed, order details are handled upon ordering, the customer will fill up a short form when making orders.

At the same time, the website provides an **Employee Sign In** entry point, typically accessible through the navigation menu or footer. Authorized employees can authenticate and access a workspace tailored to their assigned role.

The employee side uses **role-based access control (RBAC)** to ensure that users only have access to the interfaces, tools, and data appropriate to their responsibilities. Given:

- **Managers** → management dashboards and oversight, business forecasts by AI API, Employee Assignments and Schedules, Outlet Restock Schedule
- **Sales staff** → sales and order management, expense recording
- **Production staff** → baking log, update ingredients inventory, update available product stock, preparing outlet restock job

In essence, the system provides **one unified website with two primary experiences**:

**Customers → Online Storefront → Shopping → Order Filling**

**Employees → Secure Login → Role-Based Business Workspace**

The goal is to provide a seamless digital platform where customers interact with the business through the storefront while employees manage and operate the business through secure internal interfaces, all within a single integrated web application.

The name of the customer portal will be the business name **Purple Yam Malaybalay**

The name of the employee portal will be **PYRAMIS**
