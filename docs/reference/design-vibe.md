# PYRAMIS Design Vibe

## Positioning

**PYRAMIS** should feel like a premium, warm, modern bakery management platform. The aesthetic should avoid being overly corporate or overly playful, balancing operational clarity with the approachable character of a bakery brand.

> **One-line direction:**
> PYRAMIS is a premium violet-toned business dashboard with warm bakery character, combining deep orchid surfaces, soft neutral backgrounds, restrained rounded components, generous whitespace, and clear data visualization for a calm, intelligent, and trustworthy operational experience.

## Suggested Color Roles

- **Primary / Brand:** `#A83CC3` Velvet Orchid 500
- **Primary Dark / Structural:** `#43184E` Velvet Orchid 800 and `#220C27` Velvet Orchid 900
- **Secondary:** Midnight Violet and Dark Amethyst for emphasis, active states, and selected interface elements
- **Backgrounds:** Bright Snow and Dust Grey light shades
- **Surfaces:** White or very light Bright Snow tones
- **Text:** Deep Bright Snow or Dust Grey tones depending on contrast requirements
- **Accent:** Use brighter violet shades sparingly for important actions, highlights, and status indicators

Avoid making every component purple. The violet palette should establish identity while the warm neutrals provide breathing room and readability.

## Vibe Words

**Premium · Warm · Intelligent · Organized · Trustworthy · Modern · Approachable**

## Visual Character

- Use **medium-radius cards, buttons, inputs, and panels** rather than extremely rounded or sharp components.
- Use **subtle borders and low-contrast shadows** to create hierarchy without making the interface feel heavy.
- Maintain **generous spacing and whitespace** so dashboards remain easy to scan.
- Establish a **strong visual hierarchy** through typography, spacing, and restrained color contrast.
- Keep the interface **data-forward**, especially for sales, inventory, production, workforce, and forecasting screens.
- Introduce **bakery warmth** through imagery, small organic details, and soft neutral tones rather than decorative clutter.
- Use violet strategically for navigation, active states, key actions, charts, and important metrics.

## Overall Mood

The final interface should feel **calm, intelligent, organized, and premium**, while still carrying enough warmth to reflect Purple Yam Malaybalay as a real bakery business. It should look like a serious operational system without feeling cold or intimidating.
